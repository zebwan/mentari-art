Harmonic Echoes Art Exhibition

Harmonic Echoes

Abstract works by Rube Jamal

Colour and gesture as a space for personal reflection.

Harmonic Echoes presents a collection of abstract works by Rube Jamal, whose artistic practice spans more than five decades. Guided by spontaneity, emotion, and live experience, her paintings explore rhythm, movement, and emotional resonance through vibrant colours, expressive lines, and layered compositions.

Through abstraction, the exhibition invites viewers into a contemplative space where joy, sorrow, wonder, memory, and reflection unfold through colour and gesture. Rather than presenting fixed narratives, the works encourage personal interpretation and emotional connection — allowing each viewer to experience their own harmonic echoes within the space.

Exhibition notes

A living rhythm of colour, gesture, and memory.

Presented by Mentari Art Gallery, the exhibition invites visitors to experience the works through personal reflection rather than fixed narrative.

About the artist

Rube Jamal

Rube Jamal is a Malaysian abstract artist whose artistic journey spans more than five decades. A graduate of the Master of Fine Arts programme at The Maryland Institute College of Art, USA, she has been actively exhibiting since 1972. Over the years, she has participated in numerous exhibitions locally and internationally, including at Balai Seni Visual Negara, University of Malaya Art Gallery, Universiti Teknologi MARA, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia, G13 Gallery, ASWARA, Pavilion Damansara Heights, The Pennsylvania State University, and The Maryland Institute College of Art.

Working primarily in abstraction, her paintings are guided by spontaneity, emotional intuition, and lived experience. Through dynamic relationships between colour, movement, line, tone, and space, her works evoke rhythm, memory, contemplation, and emotional resonance. Her artworks are part of collections including University of Malaya Art Gallery, The New Straits Times Press, Galeri Prima, Galeri Seni Lukis Perlis, Henry Butcher Art Auctioneers, Access Alliance Asia Sdn Bhd, as well as private collections in Malaysia and abroad.

About the curator

Shafiq Ghazie

Shafiq Ghazie is a Malaysian independent curator and creative practitioner whose work explores the intersection of contemporary art, spatial experience, visual culture, and public engagement. With a multidisciplinary background spanning creative arts, media, branding, and visual communication, his curatorial approach emphasizes emotional resonance, spatial storytelling, and accessible contemporary art experiences within live and public environments.

As one of the founders of Mentari Art Gallery, he focuses on developing intimate curatorial platforms that connect emerging and established Malaysian artists with wider audiences through exhibitions, collaborations, and cultural initiatives. His practice combines contemporary curatorial sensibilities with reflective and human-centred approaches, positioning art as both an emotional encounter and a shared cultural dialogue.
