About the gallery

Contemporary art, placed within lived public space.

Mentari Art Gallery by Alami Mentari Resources is a contemporary curatorial art platform dedicated to presenting and supporting Malaysian emerging and established artists through meaningful visual and spatial experiences.

Rooted within a hotel residency environment, the gallery embraces an intimate curatorial approach that connects art with everyday public encounters, inviting hotel guests, visitors, collectors, academics, and surrounding communities into closer engagement with contemporary artistic expression.

Inspired by the meaning of Mentari — the sun as a symbol of light, warmth, energy, and new beginnings — the gallery seeks to create space where art becomes a source of reflection, dialogue, and emotional resonance.

Current exhibition

Harmonic Echoes

A contemplative presentation of abstract works by Rube Jamal, whose practice spans more than five decades. The exhibition invites viewers into a space where joy, sorrow, wonder, memory, and reflection unfold through colour and gesture.
